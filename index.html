01 / Profile

Engineering that reaches the shop floor.

I connect mechanical engineering with automation implementation—balancing function, manufacturability, maintainability and the needs of production teams.

What I do

Develop custom industrial equipment and production improvements, including assembly stations, mechanical mechanisms, pneumatic tooling and robot-ready fixtures. I coordinate with machining and fabrication partners and help bring concepts into physical systems that can be tested and refined.

Concept development3D prototypingFabrication follow-upCommissioning support

How I approach a challenge

Start with the process and constraints, design a practical solution, validate interfaces and movement, build and integrate the hardware, then iterate based on real-world behaviour. I value clear documentation, sensible design choices and collaboration between engineering, suppliers and production.

Problem solvingDesign for assemblyCross-functional teamworkContinuous improvement

Design and Analysis of Vertical Levitated Axis Wind Turbine (VLAWT)

Co-authored with Minesh Vohra · Journal of Emerging Technologies and Innovative Research (JETIR)
